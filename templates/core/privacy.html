Privacy

We keep as little as we can.

This is a demo, so use made-up details. Here is what the site stores, what it never asks for, and how to delete your account.

What we store

  • Your email, used only to log in. It is never shown to other students.
  • Your first and last name, shown publicly as your first name and last initial, for example “Marcus B.”
  • Your campus, and your class year if you add it.
  • The time you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Your password, stored only as an Argon2id hash.
  • For cooks: your bio, menus and meals. A pickup note is shown only to diners the cook has confirmed.
  • Your bookings and grocery shares, with any custom grocery terms or note you write. Only the cook sees them.
  • Your reviews, with the star rating and any text you write.
    • {% if ai_import_enabled %}
  • If you use “Paste a menu”, the text you paste is sent to an AI service, Anthropic's Claude API, to draft your dishes. This site does not store that text. Anthropic receives it and may keep it for a limited time under its own terms. Nothing else about you is sent.
    • {% endif %}

What we never ask for

  • Gender, age or date of birth.
  • Your home address or room number.
  • Photos.
  • Your own allergies or health details.
  • Card numbers, bank details or payment handles. No money moves through this site.

The site sets a session cookie to keep you logged in. It has no analytics and no trackers.

Delete your account

From your account page, choose “Delete account” and enter your current password. Then:

  • your open bookings are cancelled;
  • if you cook, your upcoming meals are cancelled and your menu is hidden;
  • your email, name and cook bio are removed;
  • the terms and notes on your bookings are removed;
  • the text of your reviews is removed.

What stays:

  • your past bookings and the star ratings of your reviews, under “Former student”, because they belong to the other student too;
  • your campus, class year and the time you confirmed you are 18 or older, on the anonymized account.

Deleting your account here does not remove text already sent to Anthropic through “Paste a menu”.
