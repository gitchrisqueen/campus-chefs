Safety

Read this before you share a meal.

Campus Chefs is a portfolio demo. The people and campuses are fictional, no real meals are served, and no money moves through this site.

  1. This is not a real service.

    Rules on home-cooked food made for other people (often called cottage-food rules) vary by state, county, city and campus. Campus Chefs does not check them, so it is a demo and not a service to use.

  2. Allergens are self-declared.

    Each cook lists the allergens in their own dishes. Nobody checks those lists, and the allergen filter only hides dishes based on what the cook declared.

  3. Severe allergies: do not rely on this site.

    If you have a severe allergy, do not rely on any listing here. Talk to the cook directly, or skip the meal.

  4. Nobody inspects the kitchens.

    No health inspector, campus office or anyone else has looked at a cook's kitchen or food handling.

  5. Grocery shares are cost-sharing, capped at the grocery cost.

    Diners chip in toward the groceries, and the shares for a meal can never add up to more than the cook's grocery estimate. Some places may still treat this as a sale of food.

This is not legal advice. If you build something like this for real, check the food and business rules where you live.
