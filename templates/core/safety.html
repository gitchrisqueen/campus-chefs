Safety

Campus Chefs is a portfolio demo. The people and campuses are made up.

  • Rules for selling or sharing home-cooked food vary by place. This is not a real service.
  • Cooks declare their own allergens. Nobody checks them.
  • If you have a severe allergy, do not rely on this site.
  • Nobody inspects the kitchens.
  • Grocery shares are cost-sharing, capped at what the groceries cost. Some places may still treat them as a sale of food. This is not legal advice.
